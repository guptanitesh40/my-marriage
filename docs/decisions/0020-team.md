# 0020 — Team: admins, managers, invitations and several weddings

Date: 2026-11-22 · Status: accepted (M12 built)

## Why

PRD TEAM-01 to TEAM-05 let owners and managers manage managers. Reviewing
the M12.0 analysis, the owner changed the model: managers must not manage
anyone, the couple must be able to give someone like a parent full
planning and team rights, and anyone may own or help plan several
weddings. These answers supersede the PRD and schema document where they
differ.

## Decisions

**Roles**

- `owner`: the bride and groom, at most two, equal, protected: nobody
  removes or changes them through the team, and an owner never becomes
  anything else by accepting an invitation.
- `admin` (new): anyone else the couple trusts. Everything a manager can
  do, plus the team (invite, remove, change admins and managers) and
  deleting events, guests, vendors and expenses (until now owners only).
  Admins cannot invite the partner, remove an owner, or archive or delete
  the wedding; the archived-wedding safety actions (unpublish, hide or
  delete photos) stay with owners.
- `manager`: plans; never sees the team page or its routes (403
  `ADMIN_REQUIRED`). They still read `GET /members` (names and roles, no
  addresses) to assign tasks.
- Owners and admins can change a member between admin and manager, but
  never their own role and never an owner's. Anyone's label can be set.
- Nobody can leave or remove themselves (`NOT_YOURSELF`); someone who no
  longer wants to help just stops using it, and an owner or admin can
  remove them.

**Several weddings per account**

- The one-active-owned-wedding rule (PRD WED-03, schema §5.1) is removed:
  migration 0018 drops `uniq_active_owned_wedding_per_user`, and creating
  or accepting no longer answers `OWNER_ACTIVE_LIMIT`. `activeOwned` is
  still written but limits nothing. A repeated "create" submission now
  creates a second wedding; the form's button is disabled while it is
  sent, which is the only guard left.
- A wedding switcher in the sidebar (and the top bar on phones) lists
  every wedding with its role and links to "Start another wedding"
  (`/onboarding?start=new`). Onboarding step 2 takes `?wedding=` so it
  invites the partner of the wedding just created.

**Invitations** (one service, `MemberInvitesService`, for every role)

- `POST /member-invites` takes `role` (`owner`, `admin`, `manager`) and an
  optional `label`. The partner: owners only, replacing a pending partner
  invitation (unchanged). Admins and managers: owners and admins.
- 409 `ALREADY_MEMBER` if the address belongs to a member with that role
  or a higher one (a manager may still be invited as the partner);
  409 `ALREADY_INVITED` if a pending invitation exists — resend it
  instead. Checked inside the transaction after the write fence, so
  simultaneous invitations to one address let exactly one through.
- `POST …/member-invites/{id}/revoke` (owners and admins; the partner's,
  owners): the links stop working at once and the email is not sent if
  still queued. `INVITE_NOT_PENDING` once accepted or declined.
- `POST /member-invites/decline` (by link) and
  `POST /member-invites/{id}/decline` (signed in as the invited address):
  status `declined`; the person can be invited again.
- Accepting is role-aware: admins and managers join with no ownership
  check; the invitation's label becomes the member's; a removed member is
  made active again; a role is never lowered.
- The email for admins and managers says what the role may do
  (`teamInviteMessage`).

**Removing someone** (`DELETE /members/{userId}`, owners and admins)

- Never an owner (`OWNER_PROTECTED`, also enforced in the write), never
  yourself. The membership is kept, marked `removed` with `removedAt` and
  `removedBy`. Access ends on their next request: every wedding route
  checks active membership, so no session needs revoking.
- Every task assigned to them, open or done, becomes unassigned in the
  same transaction (owner's answer); `completedBy` and everything they
  wrote stay.

**Audit log** (PRD TEAM-05)

- `audit_events` (schema §4.27), written by `AuditService.record` in the
  transaction of the change: `member_invite.created`, `.resent`,
  `.revoked`, `.declined`, `member.joined`, `member.updated`,
  `member.removed`. `safeDiff` holds roles and counts only — never tokens,
  addresses or email text. No reading endpoint yet.

**API additions** (besides the above)

- `GET /weddings/{id}/team`: members with address, label, joined date,
  open tasks and `isYou`, plus the latest 50 invitations with who sent
  them; owners and admins only. `PATCH /weddings/{id}/members/{userId}`
  changes role and label.

**Mobile**

- No Stitch mobile screens: the page stacks; the invite and detail
  drawers are full-height, full-width sheets on phones.

## Still open

- The workspace has no section menu under 1024 px (the sidebar is hidden);
  on phones the Team page, like the other sections, is reached by its
  address or from the overview. Not new with M12.
- Reading the audit log (an activity page) is not built.
- PRD WED-03 / schema §5.1 still describe one owned wedding per account;
  this record supersedes them.
