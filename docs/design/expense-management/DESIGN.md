## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-fidelity wedding operations and planning platform engineered specifically for modern Indian couples, families, and creative partners. Traditional wedding tools often suffer from visual cliches—such as oversaturated magenta, excessive gilded gradients, and heavy ornamental borders. In contrast, this design system treats wedding curation with the discipline, focus, and utility of modern product-led software (akin to Linear, Notion, and Stripe), married to the warmth and tactile elegance of a bespoke editorial publication.

### Personality & Tone
- **Calm Authority:** Transforms an inherently chaotic, multi-day, multi-stakeholder cultural event into a serene, structured, and manageable roadmap.
- **Warm Editorial:** Balances high-utility project management elements (timelines, budget allocations, vendor tracking, guest sequencing) with typographic warmth and generous negative space.
- **Restrained Celebration:** Employs celebration as an atmosphere rather than a decoration. Metallic and warm accents remain structural, subtle, and disciplined.

### Target Audience & Experience
Designed for contemporary couples coordinating complex, multi-event celebrations across diverse venues and families. The interface evokes quiet confidence, effortless organization, and a sense of shared hospitality without ever feeling bureaucratic or overly stark.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in an organic, architectural neutrality paired with deep botanical greens and discreet champagne undertones.

### Roles & Execution
- **Canvas Base (`#F7F5F0`):** A warm ivory that replaces cold digital white, providing an inviting paper-like backdrop for day-long organizational sessions.
- **Primary Text & Headings (`#24302D`):** A deep botanical charcoal that delivers high-contrast readability with a softer, richer optical presence than pure `#000000`.
- **Primary Actions & Focus (`#315D50`):** An architectural forest green indicating decisive actions, primary triggers, completed milestones, and primary data states.
- **Subtle Decorative Details & Accents (`#B89A70`):** A muted champagne gold applied strictly to active indicator pills, subtle highlight badges, milestones, and select metadata tags. Never used for full background fills or large text.
- **Structural Lines & Outlines (`#E9E5DC`):** A soft stone used for hair-thin borders, dividers, grid lines, and interactive table rules.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`):** Crisp white reserved for primary interactive cards, form panels, modal sheets, and dropdown surfaces, separating active work modules from the warm ivory canvas.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional contrast between literary editorial presence and high-density productivity layout.

- **Editorial Headings (Newsreader):** Brings a nuanced, bookish, and elevated optical craft to event titles, overview dashboards, ritual schedules, and couple introductions. It anchors emotional resonance without feeling dated.
- **Product Sans (Manrope):** Delivers precise, geometric legibility across nested guest tables, vendor contracts, financial ledgers, navigation bars, and interactive buttons.
- **Typographic Rules:**
  - Never set headings in heavy weights (avoid bold weights above 600 in serif titles); rely on size, tracking, and optical spacing instead.
  - Data points, counts, and currency counters use `Manrope` with tabular numbers enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined, fluid grid built on an 8pt modular cadence. Content architecture reflects high-utility SaaS workspaces while maintaining generous breathing room.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid layout maxing out at `1440px` centered canvas with `2.5rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Ideal for multi-column schedule boards, budget spreadsheets, and side-by-side vendor comparisons.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Reflows split-view workspaces into stacked tabbed views.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Complex data grids collapse into vertical sequence cards with horizontal metadata carousels.

### Layout Principles
- Dense data (such as RSVP statuses and seating charts) maintains clean row rhythm using compact vertical padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`), preserving horizontal alignment across cards.
- Strategic editorial sections (such as event agendas and summary dashboards) employ expanded whitespace (`space-xl` and above) to introduce composure.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid standard SaaS shadows or artificial gloss, this design system uses soft ambient diffusion paired with crisp structural outlines.

### Architectural Hierarchy
- **Canvas (`#F7F5F0`):** The structural foundation. Sits at the bottom of the visual stack.
- **Base Cards (`#FFFFFF`):** High-craft white surfaces sitting directly on the canvas, bounded by a 1px solid `#E9E5DC` hairline border. Elevation is indicated primarily through this color contrast rather than heavy drop shadows.
- **Floating Overlays & Menus:** Dropdowns, popovers, and sticky sub-navigation bars use a highly diffused ambient shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(36, 48, 45, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(36, 48, 45, 0.03)` wrapped in a 1px solid `#E9E5DC` boundary.
- **Modals & Drawers:** High-focus surfaces use a warm scrim (`rgba(36, 48, 45, 0.28)`) with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`. The surface maintains an elevation shadow: `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(36, 48, 45, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometric character balances architectural precision with organic softness. 

- **Primary Surfaces & Cards:** Standardize on `rounded-lg` (16px) to `rounded-xl` (20px), introducing refined softness to data-heavy SaaS views.
- **Interactive Controls:** Form fields, filter bars, segmented buttons, and chips use `rounded-md` (8px) to maintain a crisp, tool-like interaction footprint.
- **Status Badges & Avatar Containers:** Feature pill-shapes (`rounded-full`) to immediately differentiate status indicators and tags from active cards and inputs.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button:** `#315D50` forest green background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-md` (8px), height 40px, font `Manrope` (weight 600). Hover state lightens subtly to `#3A6E5F`.
- **Secondary Button:** Pure `#FFFFFF` background, hairline border in `#E9E5DC`, `#24302D` text. On hover, background shifts to `#F7F5F0`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `#315D50` text with zero border. Hover introduces an ultra-light tint (`rgba(49, 93, 80, 0.06)`).

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Default container features `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E9E5DC` border, and 8px border radius.
- Text rendered in `Manrope` (`0.9375rem`), placeholder text in `#7E8783`.
- Active focus state replaces standard blue rings with a precise, crisp 1.5px `#315D50` outline and no outer glow.

### Cards & Work Containers
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) containers resting on warm ivory (`#F7F5F0`), bounded by a single hairline border (`#E9E5DC`) with corner radius set to 16px.
- Internal padding adheres strictly to `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), housing a clear hierarchy: editorial headline (`Newsreader`), functional sub-label (`Manrope`), and discrete data module.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Pills:** Neutral background `#F7F5F0`, 1px border `#E9E5DC`, `#24302D` text, 600 weight.
- **Celebratory Milestone / VIP Indicator:** Soft champagne background (`rgba(184, 154, 112, 0.12)`), `#7D6340` text, subtle `#B89A70` border.
- **Progress / Confirmed Badge:** Soft forest tint (`rgba(49, 93, 80, 0.1)`), `#315D50` text.

### Tables & Data Grids (RSVP, Budget, Logistics)
- Borderless outer perimeter; interior separation relies solely on 1px `#E9E5DC` row dividers.
- Header row utilizes `label-sm` uppercase styling in `#7E8783` against the `#F7F5F0` background.
- Interactive rows maintain subtle hover shifts to `#FBF9F6` for scan-ability across large guest lists.

### Event & Multi-Day Timeline Modules
- A vertical left-anchored guideline in `#E9E5DC` connected by 8px circular checkpoint nodes.
- Selected / active wedding function (e.g., Sangeet, Mehendi, Ceremony) highlights the node in `#315D50` with an outer `#FFFFFF` ring, while inactive events remain `#B89A70` or `#E9E5DC`.
