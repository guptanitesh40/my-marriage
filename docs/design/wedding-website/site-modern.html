Our Story

It began in Mumbai with shared conversations tucked away in quiet coastal cafes and late evening walks along the curving sweep of Marine Drive. What started as kindred laughter quickly grew into a steadfast bond anchored by common dreams, enduring friendship, and an unmistakable sense of home in one another.

Now, surrounded by the heritage pink stone walls, open starlit courtyards, and fragrant marigold arches of Jaipur, we look forward to uniting our families and traditions. We cannot wait to celebrate this milestone together with the people who have shaped our lives.

Questions

Jaipur in mid-February enjoys pleasant, sunny afternoons (around 24°C / 75°F) and brisk evenings (dropping to 10°C / 50°F). For daytime ceremonies like Haldi, light fabrics, linens, and festive florals are ideal. For the Sangeet and Pheras, evening functions take place outdoors, so we recommend rich silks, velvets, lehengas, sherwanis, and carrying a shawl or formal wrap for comfort after sunset.
Valet parking will be provided at all ceremony locations for private vehicles and hired cabs.
Dedicated shuttle coaches will run between designated guest hotels and ceremonial venues throughout each day.
