Frequently Asked

Questions & Answers

◆
What should I wear?
Jaipur in mid-February enjoys pleasant, sunny afternoons (around 24°C / 75°F) and brisk evenings (dropping to 10°C / 50°F). For daytime ceremonies like Haldi, light fabrics, linens, and festive florals are ideal. For the Sangeet and Pheras, evening functions take place outdoors, so we recommend rich silks, velvets, lehengas, sherwanis, and carrying a shawl or formal wrap for comfort after sunset.
Is there parking at the venues?
Yes, valet parking will be available at the entrance of both Rajvilas and Royal Haveli for all arriving guests. Dedicated concierge attendants will assist with parking and buggy transit to ceremony lawns.
Will there be transport between venues?
Complimentary coach shuttles will run between designated partner hotels and celebration venues before each event starts and after dinner concludes. Shuttles depart every 20 minutes from hotel lobby concierges.
